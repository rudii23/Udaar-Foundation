About Udaar Foundation

Rooted in Compassion, Committed to Real Impact

Working directly within the slum settlements of Village Sorkha (Sector 115, Noida) to empower children and women with dignity and opportunity.

Our Mission

To break the cycle of intergenerational poverty by equipping slum children with high-quality education, dignity, and life skills.

Child-Centric Action

Our Vision

An inclusive society where every child, irrespective of background or economic status, has equal opportunity to learn, grow, and lead.

Core Focus Areas

What We Do

Inspired by benchmark non-profit architectures (Setu & OM Foundation), our 4 modular pillars address urgent grassroots vulnerabilities.

Core Education

1. Slum Literacy & Remedial Learning

Dedicated learning centers, school enrollment drives, homework clubs, and daily mentoring for first-generation learners in Village Sorkha.

Key Components:

  • Bridge & remedial classes in Hindi, English, and Mathematics.
  • "Chalo School" formal school enrollment assistance in Noida.
  • Daily homework mentoring circles led by passionate educators.
  • Values, spoken confidence, and storytelling workshops.

Beneficiaries: 350+ children aged 5–16 years.

Sponsor Child
Student Welfare

2. "Equipping Dreams" School Bag & Kit Drives

Distributing sturdy backpacks, stationery sets, notebooks, and uniforms to remove material barriers and restore student pride.

Key Components:

  • Ergonomic, weather-proof school backpacks.
  • Full academic stationery (notebooks, geometry sets, art supplies).
  • Seasonal footwear, socks, and winter sweater distribution drives.
  • Reading kits and slum library box setup.

Milestone: 1,500+ kits distributed across Delhi-NCR.

Sponsor Kit (₹500)
Health & Nutrition

3. Healthcare & Nutrition Camps

Regular pediatric health checkups, dental/eye screenings, micronutrient meals, and adolescent hygiene/menstrual awareness workshops.

Key Components:

  • BMI & malnutrition monitoring with iron/vitamin supplementation.
  • Dental hygiene and refractive eye testing by partner doctors.
  • Adolescent menstrual health workshops & free sanitary pad drives.
  • Clean drinking water & preventive dengue/seasonal disease awareness.

Impact: 10,000+ meals & 800+ young girls supported.

Support Camp
Livelihoods

4. Youth Skilling & Women Empowerment

Vocational workshops, computer literacy, spoken English, and micro-entrepreneurship guidance for mothers and youth.

Key Components:

  • Tailoring, stitching, and handicraft training for mothers.
  • Basic computer operations & MS Office literacy for teens.
  • Bank account opening, Jan Dhan linkage, and financial literacy.
  • Interview readiness and career counseling.

Reach: 180+ women & youth trained in Noida.
